For You, Dibya

Once, we were two people who found so much happiness in simply having each other. We made memories, shared little jokes, dreamed about tomorrow, and had moments when the world felt right because we were together. I will never pretend those days meant nothing. They meant more to me than I can explain.

But somewhere along the way, things changed, and something beautiful became something we could no longer hold together. It hurts to accept that a story can be real and meaningful, yet still come to an end. I wish things had turned out differently, but I don't want to turn our memories into blame or bitterness.

Thank you for everything you gave to my life. Thank you for the love, the laughter, the lessons, and the moments when you made me feel happier than I knew I could be. You gave me a kind of love I sometimes felt I didn't deserve, and I will always be grateful for that.

Maybe I wasn't the right person for you, or maybe I couldn't become the person you needed. I am sorry for the ways I fell short. I hope you never think you were difficult to love. You deserve a love that feels peaceful, certain, gentle, and safe.

If our paths move in different directions, I truly hope life is kind to you. I hope you achieve everything you dream of, laugh until your heart feels light, and find happiness in places you never expected. Even if I am no longer part of that happiness, I will sincerely wish it for you.

Some people become a chapter, not the whole book. You will always be a chapter I read with a grateful heart. Thank you for being part of my life, Dibya. Take care of yourself, and be happy. 🤍

With gratitude, Bhabesh
